# SimplyPrint @ SoC

SimplyPrint @ SoC provides a hassle-free experience with SoC printing without you ever touching the command line.

## Download

[Download for macOS](https://github.com/ppannawitt/print-soc/releases/download/v1.0.0/Print-at-SoC-Universal.dmg)

Requires macOS 13 or later. Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. Windows is coming soon.

1. Open the downloaded DMG.
2. Drag the app into **Applications**.
3. Open the app from **Applications**.

## Set up your account

1. Open **Account** in the app.
2. Enter your SoC username and password, then choose **Save**.
3. If you need an SSH key, follow the [SSH-key setup guide](https://ppannawitt.github.io/print-soc/ssh-keys.html). Register it manually with SoC, then select the key in **Account**.
4. Choose **Connect**. If you are off campus, connect to the NUS VPN if needed.

You need an active mySoC account with printing access. The [printing guide](https://ppannawitt.github.io/print-soc/printing.html) includes account and connection help.

## Print a PDF

1. Open **Print** and choose your PDF.
2. Select your printer and check its location.
3. Review the preview, pages, copies, and other print settings.
4. Choose **Print** and confirm your selection.
5. Check **Jobs** or **Queues**, then collect your pages.

Use **Printers** to browse locations and available printers. To cancel a waiting job, find it in **Jobs** and choose **Cancel**. If a submission is uncertain, check the queue before printing again.
